How to play

  1. Walk your ship deckplan with WASD, arrow keys, or by tapping/clicking anywhere.
  2. Stand near an indoor station and press E / Space (or tap it) to work—tap again inside the cyan arc (55%–82%) to Overcharge!
  3. Walk down the ship's Airlock ramp onto the surface and press E / tap an exterior node to instantly grab 1 resource, then haul it back inside to deposit it (or press Q to drop it).
  4. Keep Power and Oxygen above zero and Crew Dose below 100% for 5 sols (30s each).

The trade-offs

  • ☀️ 120s (4-Sol) Day/Night cycle: 60s daylight (100% solar output) and 60s night (20% solar output).
  • 🎒 You can only carry one resource at a time outdoors, and heavy samples slightly slow your sprint unless you are the EVA Specialist.
  • ⚡ Raising shields and scrubbing CO₂ both draw from your ship's battery bank.
  • ☢️ Radiation dose never resets on its own—treat exposure in the Medical Bay.

Real NASA data: solar storm intensity scales with the number of CMEs logged by NASA DONKI in the last 30 days. If offline, storms are simulated.

📖 How to play

Welcome, cadet! You command a landed starship outpost where every engineering choice is a trade-off.

  • 🛸 6 Deckplan Ships: Each spacecraft features its own custom interior floor plan, boarding ramp, and performance perks.
  • 🎩 Role Gear: Every role wears a signature space-grade hat on both your briefing hologram and outpost astronaut sprite.
  • 🎒 1-Item Physical EVA Carry: Grab exterior ice cores, meteorite fragments, fuel cells, or telemetry pods (1 at a time) and carry them back inside the Airlock to stow! Press Q to drop a carried item if a storm hits.
  • ⚡ Power vs. shield & air: electromagnetic shielding and CO₂ scrubbers both consume electricity.

A sol is one planetary day (30 seconds). A CME is a solar plasma burst, and dose tracks cumulative crew radiation exposure.
